## Paragraph (blog intro / Product Hunt)

> OpenAgentPay is an open-source abstraction layer that sits between AI agents and the payment world. Just as LiteLLM unified 100+ LLM providers behind one interface, OpenAgentPay unifies **wallets** (61 connectors — EVM, CEX, MPC, Move, Cosmos, Bitcoin), **payment protocols** (22 adapters — Coinbase x402, Google AP2, Stripe MPP, OpenAI ACP, W3C Web Monetization, IETF GNAP, Lightning L402, and more), and **agent frameworks** (35 plugins — LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen, Strands…) behind one consistent API. Switch any of the three with a single config line; your agent's business logic never changes. It ships a 7-layer governance guardrail, a federated conformance certifier, SDKs in 6 languages, and 4,114 passing tests — live on AWS today.

## Three-bullet "why now"

- **Agents are starting to transact** — x402, Google AP2, OpenAI ACP, Stripe MPP all launched payment standards in the last year. Fragmentation is guaranteed.
- **No one owns the abstraction** — every wallet/protocol/framework integration is hand-rolled today. That's exactly the gap LiteLLM filled for inference.
- **Neutral & open** — Apache-2.0, self-hostable, framework-agnostic kernel, federated self-certification. The ecosystem can grow its own connectors.
